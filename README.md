# Day 2 — Traditional AI, Generative AI and Combined AI in Aviation

## Overview

This lab explores the differences between **Traditional AI, Generative AI, and Combined AI** in the context of aviation management. The activities focus on understanding how different AI capabilities can support airline and airport operations while keeping important decisions under appropriate human control.

## Objectives

- Understand the difference between Traditional AI and Generative AI.
- Classify aviation-management tasks according to their AI requirements.
- Understand how Traditional AI and Generative AI can work together.
- Identify situations requiring human judgement.
- Understand AI hallucination and information-verification risks.# DAY-2
